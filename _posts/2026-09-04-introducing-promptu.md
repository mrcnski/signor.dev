Did you know that you can talk to AI using prompts?

What if I told you that prompts can be composed using building blocks?  Or that
you can generate entire prompts with just a few keystrokes?

You may think it's impossible, but that's exactly what I've done with my
revolutionary project [Promptu](https://github.com/mrcnski/promptu).

`Commit, add tests, commit, don't push`.  With Promptu, just five key presses.

{% include image.html noborder=true name="introducing-promptu/screenshot.png" alt="Promptu screenshot." width="400" %}

I know, you can hardly believe it.  Or maybe you're thinking about some cake in
the fridge.  Stay with me, because it gets better.

You can remove instructions, rearrange them, even *edit* them - so you don't
have to get everything right the first time.  Because unlike me, you probably
make mistakes.

And that's OK.  Promptu lets you turn your booboos into works of art.

Then your masterpiece of a prompt gets funneled straight into an AI data center.
Incredible.

Unleash your power, one keystroke at a time.
[Promptu](https://github.com/mrcnski/promptu) is here, and it's free.

(And there's an [Emacs package](https://github.com/mrcnski/promptu.el)!)
